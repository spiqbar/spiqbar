## Selected projects

**CAS-Net — Multimodal Sexism Detection (EXIST 2026)**
Deep learning architecture fusing text (XLM-RoBERTa), image (CLIP), and physiological signals (EEG, heart rate) to detect sexism in memes, following the Learning with Disagreement paradigm. Integrated a Legal RAG module that grounds predictions in EU/UN regulatory text, boosting F1-Macro by up to 15% (0.7102 on the official test set).

**Financial Regime Shift Detection**
Multi-task deep learning model (Conv1D-BiLSTM with a Bayesian uncertainty layer) to detect structural regime shifts in financial time series, localizing changepoints to within 16.85 days RMSE.

**Valencia Smart City: Urban Intelligence & XAI**
Interactive Shiny platform predicting and explaining daily NO2/PM10 air pollution in Valencia, combining Random Forest with real-time explainability (`iBreakDown`) and an automated fairness audit checking whether predictions stay consistent across different urban zones.

**Neurodegenerative Disease Detection**
Multivariate statistical analysis (PCA, K-Medoids clustering, PLS-DA) to differentiate Alzheimer's, Parkinson's, and healthy cognitive profiles, with association rule mining (Apriori) to validate which cognitive tests hold up as diagnostic biomarkers.
